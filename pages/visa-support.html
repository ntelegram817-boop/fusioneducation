Our Visa Application Process

1

Consultation & Eligibility Check

We assess your background, qualifications, and goals to recommend the best visa type and pathway for you.

Timeline: 1-2 days

2

Document Preparation

We collect and organize all required documents including educational certificates, financial documents, and personal information.

Timeline: 5-7 days

3

Application Submission

We submit your complete application package to the appropriate Japanese government office or designated institution.

Timeline: Same day

4

Processing & Interview

We monitor your application progress and prepare you thoroughly for any interviews with visa officers.

Timeline: 2-4 weeks

5

Visa Approval & Pre-Departure

Upon approval, we provide pre-departure orientation including flights, accommodation, cultural training, and emergency support contacts.

Timeline: 1-2 weeks before departure

Documents Required

Personal Documents

  • Passport
  • Birth Certificate
  • Photos (4x6cm)
  • Police Clearance

Educational Documents

  • SSC Certificate
  • HSC Certificate
  • Degree (if applicable)
  • JLPT Certificate

Financial Documents

  • Bank Statements
  • Sponsor Proof
  • Tax Returns
  • Employment Letter
